How the value is worked out

implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × maturity × breadth

  • Peer multiple, three tiers. The median market-cap-to-TVL ratio of the live-token protocols in the same category (8+ peers), else the median of its category family — Trading, Lending, Yield, Stable & RWA, Infra — else the snapshot-wide median. On this snapshot no row falls back to the global median. No flat multiple, no hand-picked names.
  • Growth, asymmetric. The 7-day TVL change, clamped to −80% … +300%. A rise counts at 0.60, a fall bites at 0.90: declines are stickier than rallies.
  • Maturity, saturating. How long the protocol has run with no token recorded: 0.70 at day zero, rising toward 1.20 and flattening — each extra year adds less than the last.
  • Breadth, diminishing. 3% per chain it runs on, on a square-root scale up to eight chains: more chains help, the ninth adds almost nothing.
  • Radar score & confidence. Every row also carries a 0–100 score (70% implied value on a log scale, 30% momentum) and a confidence — High, Medium or Low — from direct peers, a known listing date and a reported 7-day change.

A protocol is listed as pre-token when its record carries no token — no market cap, no CoinGecko id, no symbol. That is what the record says, never proof it has none. Live-token protocols are shown apart, with the market’s own values, never mixed into the ranking.
